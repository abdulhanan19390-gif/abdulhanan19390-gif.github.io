About Qissa

Qissa is a small, independent blog covering everyday life, technology, culture, and travel. We publish short, well-considered reads a few times a week — nothing that demands an hour, nothing that disappears from memory in five minutes either.

The name means "story" or "tale" — the kind told in passing, not the kind announced. That's the tone we're aiming for: observational, specific, and unhurried.

We're an independent publication with no outside newsroom behind us, which means every piece is written, edited, and published by a small team who read each other's drafts before anything goes up.

Have a story idea, a correction, or just a thought on something we published? Get in touch — details on the contact page.
